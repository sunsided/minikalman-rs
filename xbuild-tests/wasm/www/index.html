Tracking a moving target

A radar at the bottom centre measures range and bearing to a target passing by. Range noise spreads a measurement along the line of sight; bearing noise spreads it sideways, more so the further away the target is. Together they make a crescent in x/y. Both filters approximate it with a Gaussian ellipse: the Extended filter by linearizing at its estimate, the Unscented filter by pushing sigma points through the measurement function. Both run on the same measurements.

— true path ∙ measurement uncertainty — EKF estimate, 2σ ellipse — UKF estimate, 2σ ellipse
